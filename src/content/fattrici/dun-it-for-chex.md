description_en: "Dun It For Chex is one of the most influential and celebrated broodmares in the history of reining. Born in 1996 by Hollywood Dun It out of Silver N Blue Chex, she never competed in the show pen but proved to be an exceptional producer, becoming in 2019 only the second mare in history to achieve NRHA Million Dollar Dam status. Inducted into the NRHA Hall of Fame in 2018, her extraordinary offspring have surpassed $1.26 million in lifetime earnings, featuring legendary champions such as Dun It For Whizkey (NRHA Futurity L4 Open Reserve Champion), Starbucks Sidekick, Masked Gun, and Hey Roy. Owned by Cardinal Hill Training Center, she left an indelible mark on global breeding before passing away in 2019."
